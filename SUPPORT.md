# Getting help

- **Usage questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mustafakbaser/agentcheck/discussions).
- **A finding that is wrong:** open a [false positive report](https://github.com/mustafakbaser/agentcheck/issues/new?template=false-positive.yml).
- **A crash or other bug:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/mustafakbaser/agentcheck/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
- **Security issues:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.

agentcheck is maintained by one person in their spare time. Issues with a minimal reproduction
are answered first.
